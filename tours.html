How a booking works

Four steps, no
call centre

01

You ask

Send the date and the number of people. We reply with what is actually free, from the same person every time.

02

We check the tide

Lagoons and hongs only open around low water. If your date does not work for the route, we will say so before you pay.

03

Deposit, not the lot

Twenty per cent holds the boat. The balance is settled at the pier, in cash or by card.

04

Weather call at 18:00

The night before, we confirm or move you. A cancelled trip is refunded in full, always.
