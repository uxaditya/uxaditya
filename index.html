Who takes you out

Local captains,
eleven-seat boats,
no megaphones.

Andamana started with two long-tails on Tonsai beach and a simple rule: leave before the fleet. Sixteen years on, every skipper on our roster grew up on the water they sail, and every trip caps out at what a single boat can carry comfortably.

We publish real departure times rather than optimistic ones, we cancel when the sea says so, and we would rather move your booking than run a bad day out. Marine park fees are listed separately because that money is not ours.
